MDWPhys: Lightweight Temporal-Frequency Dynamic Modeling for Noise-Robust rPPG Estimation

Chentao Xu, Zhaoyang Yang*

IEEE Sensors Journal

Abstract

A lightweight spatial, temporal, and frequency-domain denoising network for non-stationary noise caused by head motion, facial expressions, and lighting changes. It combines multidirectional differences, spatial attention aggregation, gated DeltaNet modeling, and learnable wavelet soft-thresholding.

Moderate Revision

PhysLNN: A Temporally Enhanced Liquid Neural Network for Robust Heart Rate Estimation Based on Remote Photoplethysmography

Chentao Xu, Zhaoyang Yang*, Huamin Ge, Yanfei Shen

Physiological Measurement

Abstract

Differential normalization, 3D-CNN, and differential convolution enhance subtle physiological features. LTC / CfC liquid modules with sparse NCP connections model continuous-time dynamics, showing strong generalization in cross-dataset experiments across three datasets.
